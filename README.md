# Taking Tobb Technologies Local

## A social media campaign to increase brand awareness and attract new clients

### Project overview

This project proposes a practical digital marketing campaign to improve the visibility of Tobb Technologies, explain its services to a wider audience, and encourage potential clients to make contact. The campaign uses TikTok as its main short-form video platform, supported by Instagram and Facebook. Improvement on local demonstrations.

### The opportunity

A possible growth opportunity for Tobb Technologies is to strengthen its local presence and make more people aware of what the company offers. People who have not previously interacted with Tobb may not know about its IT services, projects, training activities, or the value it could provide to organisations.

This proposal treats limited visibility as an opportunity to investigate and address; it does not assume that the company has no existing marketing activity.

### Proposed idea

Run a simple, repeatable social media campaign featuring short videos and posts that: introduce Tobb Technologies and its services, show practical examples of technology work and projects, where permission has been granted, share short, useful technology tips, highlight training activities and company achievements. Invite viewers to visit the company website or contact the team. And make local events more interesting and doable through online means 

### Target audience

-   Local small and medium-sized businesses that may need IT services or
    support.
-   Organisations looking for technology solutions or service providers.
-   Students and young people interested in technology and training
    opportunities.
-   Potential partners who may be interested in working with Tobb  Technologies.

### Business value

The campaign aims to improve awareness, increase visits to Tobb's online channels, generate relevant enquiries, and create opportunities for new client relationships. South African digital research indicates that social media has a substantial potential audience. DataReportal estimated 29.1 million social media user identities in South Africa in October 2025. It also reported TikTok advertising tools indicated a potential adult audience of 29.1 million in late 2025. These are platform advertising estimates, not confirmed counts of unique active users (DataReportal, 2026).

### Campaign approach

1.  We start by making the profile description, logo, contact details, and
    website link.
2.  Plan and produce short vertical videos with clear captions.
3.  We can share the company through local means and showcasing.
4.  Publish content consistently and adapt suitable videos for TikTok videos and Instagram
    Reels and Facebook.
5.  Respond professionally to comments and direct enquiries.
6.  Review analytics weekly and improve content based on results.
7.  Improve the local events handled 

### Suggested content themes

-   **Introduction of Thobbs:** a short introduction to the company and what it does.
-   **Technology in practice:** a permitted demonstration of a service, project, or technical task.
-   **Quick tech tip:** a useful, easy-to-understand technology tip.
-   **Behind the scenes:** an appropriate look at the team's work or learning activities.
-   **What Tobb technologies can help:** a clear explanation of a service and how to
    enquire.
-   **What Tobb technologies events can improve :** Improvement on local events and incentives 
    
    

### How Tobb could use this idea

Tobb can use the proposal as a starting point for a one-month pilot. The company should approve all claims, images, client references, staff appearances, and contact details before publication. The campaign canthen be adjusted using engagement and enquiry data.
