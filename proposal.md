# Proposal: Taking Tobb Technologies Local

## A social media campaign to increase brand awareness and attract new clients

## 1. Introduction

Proposal for this issue involves Tobb Technologies increasing their
local visibility through a social media campaign. The platform to be
used is TikTok, which will feature content that can also be used on
Instagram and Facebook. The campaign involves the use of video clips,
information concerning technological issues, and contact information,
which will make it easier for people to understand the activities of
Tobb and inquire about their services.

This opportunity arises from my observations: Tobb would do well by increasing their visibility among people and organisations that are unaware of their services and activities. This is an opportunity to investigate further and not a claim that Tobb is currently invisible in terms of marketing.

## 2. Marketing opportunity

Although an organisation may be providing good services, it may miss
out on customers if they do not know what services the organisation
provides. The problem with Tobb Technologies is that people might not
associate the company with any IT support, technology services, or
technology partners if they do not know much about the organisation.

The advantage would be to utilise digital platforms to educate people
about the company, its capabilities, and how people can contact it.
This would allow Tobb to reach out to people who may not have known
the organisation otherwise.

## 3. Justification

Social media enables an organisation to communicate to its masses and share
information continuously. According to the Digital 2026 report of
DataReportal, there were 29.1 million social media user identities in
South Africa by October 2025. It was also found that TikTok's
advertising resources indicated that there could be an adult advertising
audience of 29.1 million in late 2025 (DataReportal, 2026). This is the
information about the reach and social media identities; it should not be
considered as a count of unique individuals or an assurance that the
campaign will reach those many people.

TikTok for Business suggests using vertical mobile-first videos, capturing
attention instantly, communicating benefits and adding a call-to-action.
According to the small business video guide of TikTok for Business,
videos should be short and dynamic (TikTok for Business, n.d.). The
strategy is well suited for the campaign to explain Tobb's services in
simple visual examples.

## 4. Proposed solution

Develop a one-month pilot campaign titled **Taking Tobb Technologies
Local**. Short videos would be published on TikTok, while Instagram
Reels and Facebook would serve as supplementary distribution platforms.

The content would revolve around five topics:

1.  **Introduction of Thobbs:** Introduce the company, its mission, and the services
    it offers.
2.  **Technology in action:** Present an approved demonstration of a
    service, project, or technical task.
3.  **Tech tip:** Provide one practical piece of advice regarding
    technology or IT in short video format.
4.  **Behind the scenes:** Showcase the selected moments of the team's
    work, learning, or preparation process, with permission from the
    participants.
5.  **What Tobb technologies works with** Explain a particular service, specify
    the type of clients who may benefit from it, and suggest how to
    make an enquiry.

Each post will contain a short caption, relevant hashtags, use such as "Visit our website to
find out more" or "Contact Tobb Technologies to discuss your IT
needs" at the end of videos and profile. The company should be asked to confirm the web address and contact information prior to the publication.

## 5. Target audience

Campaign will target the following:
- SMEs which may require IT services or assistance.
- Organizations which are searching for technology solutions or technology service provider.
- Students and younger generation who are passionate about technology, skill and training.
- Potential partners.

Content to be changed as per the target audience such as when content will be directed towards the business people then it will have a brief
description regarding what service is going to resolve their problem. In case of students,
training opportunities which are Tobb approved should be promoted.

## 6. Implementation plan

### Week 1: Preparation

-   Confirm the services and messages Tobb wants to promote.
-   Identify the main audience and the campaign's contact destination.
-   Set up or review the company's social media profiles and ensure
    branding and contact details are consistent.
-   Prepare a content calendar and obtain permission for any people,
    client material, logos, or workplace footage used.

### Week 2: Create and publish

-   Produce a small batch of short vertical videos.
-   Add captions and clear on-screen text.
-   Publish the first posts and share suitable versions on Instagram and
    Facebook.
-   Monitor comments and respond to genuine enquiries.

### Week 3: Build engagement

-   Publish more technology tips, service explanations, and approved
    behind-the-scenes content.
-   Invite viewers to visit the website or contact the company.
-   Review which topics receive meaningful engagement and questions.

### Week 4: Evaluate and improve

-   Compare the performance of different content themes.
-   Record website visits and enquiries that can reasonably be linked to
    the campaign.
-   Identify the strongest posts and recommend what to repeat, change,
    or stop.
-   Present the results and a recommendation on whether to continue the
    campaign.

## 7. Resources required

The pilot could start off with a low budget by utilising a mobile phone,
video editing software, internet and some time from an authorised person
to plan and publish content. What Tobb will additionally require is -
authorisation to promote the offered services; - access to brand logos
and authorised contact details; - permission from staff members,
clients or anyone featured on their video; - appointed person for handling
comments; - optionally another separate budget for paid advertisements.

No sensitive information about the client, password and internal systems
should be published by the company. All offered services need to be
verified by Tobb prior to publishing.

## 8. Expected benefits

This campaign will have the following objectives: - increase awareness of Tobb Technologies and its products; - make the company easy to find on the Internet; - drive more people interested in the company to its website or contact page; - generate opportunities for business inquiries and cooperation; - establish trust by demonstrating approved samples of the company’s work; - learn which messages and services are popular among the audience.

These are expected results, not guarantees. The one-month pilot will help understand if the chosen strategy works.
## 9. Measuring success

Before publishing, Tobb should record its current social media and
website performance as a baseline. At the end of each week, the campaign
manager should record:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Measure                             What it shows
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Video views and reach               How many times content was viewed
                                      and how widely it was distributed

  Average watch time or completion    Whether viewers stayed to watch the
  rate                                videos

  Likes, comments, shares, and saves  Whether the content encouraged
                                      interaction

  Profile visits and link clicks      Whether viewers took a step towards
                                      learning more

  Website visits from campaign links  Whether social content directed
                                      people to the website

  Relevant enquiries                  Whether potential clients or
                                      partners made contact
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The main business measure should be the number of relevant enquiries
received, rather than views alone. Tobb can use a trackable link or ask
new enquirers how they heard about the company. At the end of the pilot,
compare the results with the baseline and decide whether to continue,
adjust the content, or test paid advertising.

## 10. Conclusion

A social media campaign is an effective means for Tobb Technologies to take
advantage of the opportunity that exists with regard to increasing visibility.
Through TikTok videos backed up by Instagram and Facebook posts, Tobb will be able to showcase its services, show its capabilities, provide relevant information, and direct those who may be interested to get in touch. The fact that this campaign will only run for one month ensures that it remains practical and measurable.
